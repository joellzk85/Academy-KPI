# Clients, client history, trainers, venues, To-do, Google Calendar

Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-21. Detailed history of what was built and agreed. Read only when working on this area.

- **Client history (built 2026-10-18; Joel's spec, with his yes to two details):** every touch point with a client on the client page, a colour dot for how recently each client was contacted, "Follow-up by" on every follow-up, and @client tags in To-dos. Logic in `src/lib/touchPoints.ts` (pure); live data in `src/lib/useClientHistory.ts` (`useTouchPoints`, `useContactLimits`, `useTeamList`).
  - **Touch points timeline** (client page, `Timeline` in `ClientManager.tsx`), newest first, built each time from what's recorded (`clientTimeline`): deal created (date the client asked), follow-up notes, appointments booked and held, proposals sent (with the deal's proposal files, openable), quotations (draft and sent; sent ones have **Download PDF**, made from the locked copy), "Quotation sent another way" (the deal's date, when no sent quotation is on record), deals won and lost (reason and explanation), course outlines on the client's deals, touch points added on the client, and To-dos tagged with the client. Each entry: date, what happened, who, how, next follow-up and by whom, and the deal (tap opens it in Deals; reps see other people's deals read-only, as before). Filters: All, Touch points and follow-ups, Proposals and quotations, Deals, To-dos. Opening a quotation or outline follows its own permissions (owner, shared with, Admin, Management); otherwise a short note says who can. Only the latest note on an open deal, or a client follow-up not yet followed up, is marked overdue (`entry.open`).
  - **What counts as contact** (Joel agreed): follow-up notes, logged appointments, proposals sent, quotations sent, deals won, the date a new deal's client asked, touch points (including To-do "What happened?"), and (since 2026-10-19) sent course outlines. Drafts (quotations and outlines), booked appointments, To-dos and lost deals show on the timeline (dashed) but don't count.
  - **Add touch point** (`TouchPointForm`): date (default today, not in the future), how (call/WhatsApp/email/visit/other), contact person (from the client's contacts), what happened (required), next follow-up date (optional) and **Follow-up by** (any active team member, defaults to me; needed with a date). Saved in **`client_touch_points/{id}`** (`clientId, date, how, contactId, contactName, text, nextDate, followUpById, followUpByName, at, byId, byName, source manual/todo, taskId`). Delete: the person who added it, or Management. The wording is always "touch point".
  - **A client follow-up** stays open until something newer that counts is recorded with that client (`touchFollowUpOpen`). Open ones show in the Follow-up by person's **My home → My follow-ups** ("Client follow-up ...", red once the date passes; click opens the client page via `nav.openClient` → `openClientId` on `RepDetailDashboard`; Management without a workspace opens it in the first teammate's workspace).
  - **Top of the client page:** "Last touch point: 12 Oct 2026 by Alif (call)" with the dot, and "Next follow-up: ... by ..." (the soonest of the open deals' follow-up dates and open client follow-ups; red when overdue). Both worked out, never typed.
  - **Deal follow-up notes have Follow-up by** (`FollowUpForm`, defaults to the deal owner): saved on the note (`followUpById/Name`) and on the deal (`followUpById/Name`, next to `followUpDate`), and in the History line. Deals where you're Follow-up by appear in your My follow-ups even if you don't own them. Older notes fall back to the deal owner. Only the owner, Admin and Management log notes on a deal (unchanged); someone else named Follow-up by records what happened as a touch point on the client.
  - **Colour dot** (`ContactBadge`/`ContactDot`, always with words): green up to 30 days, amber up to 90, red after, grey "Never". The limits are **`config/client_contact_colours`** (`green`, `amber`), set on **Management → Follow-up rules** (card "Client contact colours"; Management only, members read).
  - **Client list:** columns Client, Last touch point (dot, "12 days ago", date by who (how)), Next follow-up, Deals (stacked on phones). Sort: client name A to Z (default), longest without a touch point (never first), most recently contacted, next follow-up soonest. Filters: colour counts at the top ("Green 24, Amber 11, Red 7, Never 3", tap to filter), All clients / My clients (clients on my deals, or follow-ups and touch points I'm named on; Admin and Management also get "<name>'s clients"), not contacted in 30/60/90 days, Follow-up by a person. The same dot is under the client name in the Deals panel and the work queue panel (`ClientLastTouch`).
  - **Open To-dos** box at the top of the client page (To-dos tagged with it and not done; click opens the To-do).
  - **Notes** box (`NotesBox`): lasting facts, anyone edits in place, "Last changed by ..." (`notesById/notesByName/notesAt` on the client). It left the Add/Edit client form, so it has one home.

- **Google Calendar (built 2026-10-17; Joel chose one shared company calendar, synced both ways):**
  - **How it connects:** the CRM **server** uses its own Google identity: the App Hosting service account, via the Cloud Run metadata server with the calendar scope. Nobody signs in to Google, which fits the no-emails login.
    - Management shares the company calendar with that address ("Make changes to events") and saves the Calendar ID in the CRM. The ID is in `config/google_calendar`, which only the server writes.
    - The old pop-up Google sign-in (Firebase Google provider, hardcoded nextacademy24/lzk.joel emails, attendee email invites) was removed.
  - **Server:** `serverCalendar.ts`, registered in `server.ts`, with these routes (all need a logged-in member; settings is Management only):
    - `/api/calendar/status`: shows the address to share the calendar with, for Management.
    - `/api/calendar/settings`: tests the calendar, then saves it.
    - `/api/calendar/events`: Google events for a date range. The CRM's own synced items are left out.
    - `/api/calendar/sync`: CRM to Google, at most once a minute unless forced.
    - Errors come in plain language: API not switched on, calendar not shared, not the live site.
  - **What goes to Google** (`src/lib/calendarSync.ts`, pure and tested):
    - CRM calendar events, with tagged teammates' names in the description.
    - Deal appointments, booked or held, at their time.
    - Won deals' training dates as all-day events, with programme, venue, trainer (the same rules as the deal panel), participants and owner.
    - Each has a fixed Google event id (`googleIdFor`), so there are never duplicates. What was sent is kept in `calendar_sync/{googleId}` (server-only) with a fingerprint, so unchanged items are skipped and removed ones are deleted from Google. Malaysia time.
  - **When:** the app syncs on load and every 5 minutes, and forces a sync right after adding or deleting a CRM calendar event (`refreshGoogleCalendar` in `App.tsx`). So deal changes reach Google within about 5 minutes, or straight away with "Sync now".
  - **Google to CRM:** Google events show on both calendars with a "Google" tag. They are read-only in the CRM ("Open in Google"; deleting is refused).
  - **Connected 2026-10-17** to the team's **"Next Academy"** calendar (owner tiqassr@gmail.com, shared with the whole team). Its Calendar ID is `eb83a845…@group.calendar.google.com`, shared with `firebase-app-hosting-compute@gen-lang-client-0779528352.iam.gserviceaccount.com` as "Make changes and see all event details". The Calendar API was already enabled. The first sync added 2 items.
  - **Training time (Joel, 2026-10-17):** read from the deal's quotation **Time** (sent, else newest draft). It can be changed on the deal (`deal.trainingTime`, via "Change" or "Use the quotation's time" in `TrainingTimeBox.tsx`) in the Deals panel and the Work queue panel. Logic is in `src/lib/trainingTime.ts`: `trainingTimeOf`, plus `parseTimeRange` for "9:00 AM – 5:00 PM", "9am-5pm", "09:00 - 17:00", "9.00am to 5.00pm", "1-5pm". The Google training event uses that time; if there is no time, or it can't be read, the event is all day. Since 2026-10-19 a training of several days (the deal's `trainingDays`) shows on every day (see "Training days on deals").
  - **Full calendar** ("Add event / full calendar" on My home) now opens full width under the calendar and Performance row (`showFullCalendar` in `MyHome.tsx`), instead of inside the narrow calendar card.
  - **UI:** `src/components/GoogleCalendarBar.tsx` on the full calendar shows status, last sync and Sync now. For Management it also shows the 3 setup steps, the address to share with (with Copy), and the Calendar ID box (Save and test; blank = disconnect).

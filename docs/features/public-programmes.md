# Public programmes

Read only when working on this area.

## What Joel asked (2026-10-28)

"We also will be hosting public programs: HRDC claimable, pay directly, mix payment. 1 public program is attended by multiple different companies." Decisions:

- One **Public programme** record per run, plus one **normal deal per company** that joins, owned by the rep who sold it. Each rep keeps their own Closing RM, KPIs and commission.
- Each company sends its own number of people, and has its own funding type (HRDC claimable, Direct pay, HRDC + top-up).
- Runs are on **fixed dates announced in advance**.
- Same list price per pax for everyone, but sometimes a bulk or special price: that is a discount a manager approves (the existing **Request discount** on the quotation).
- Trainer profile, course outline and venue are the same records as for in-house trainings (picked from Trainers, By title, Venues). Nothing new is typed.
- Built in two batches. **Batch 1 (sales, done 2026-10-28):** the run, the list, joining, seats, price check, date change, cancel and move. **Batch 2 (operations and money, not built):** logistics, costs, trainer PO, photos and one attendance list once on the run; each company's own grant, invoice, HRDC claim and top-up on its deal; one P&L per run; HR copies of the report with only their own staff.

## Batch 1: how it works

- **Where:** Deals → **Public programmes** (button next to Costing estimator). Everyone sees the runs; Admin and Management set them up (**New public programme**), **Change** them, and **Cancel or move companies**. Tabs: Upcoming (soonest first), Past, Cancelled; search by title, venue or trainer; pages.
- **A run:** title (from By title; it sets Classroom training or Team building), optional course outline of that title, training date and length, venue, trainer, list price per pax, seat limit (blank = none), minimum pax to run (blank = none), notes, History (add-only).
- **Seats line:** confirmed (won) pax in dark, sold so far (every company not lost or deleted) in light, a mark at the minimum. States: Taking sign-ups, Confirmed to run (won pax reach the minimum), Below minimum (training within 14 days and not enough won pax), Training on now, Training done, Cancelled.
- **Joining:** on a run, **Add a company** opens New deal with the run picked; or New deal → **Kind of training: Public programme** → pick the run (date · title · price · seats left). The run fills in the title, type and dates (locked on the deal). The deal value follows list price × pax while it still equals that (or is blank). Saving refuses: no pax, more pax than seats left ("Ask Admin or Management to raise the seat limit"), or a value below list price × pax without an approved discount ("create the quotation at that price and click Request discount"); Management may set any price. The rest of the deal journey is unchanged (proposal, quotation, Won).
- **On the deal:** a "Public programme" chip on the Deals row; Details shows the run's dates, companies and pax. A won deal can't change run in Edit (locked with the value).
- **Changing a run's date:** a short "why" is needed when companies have joined. Open deals move to the new date at once (History line); won deals get a **Change date** request in the person's name, approved by a manager as usual (never their own). Refused while any won company already has a change waiting ("Approve or withdraw it on the deal first").
- **Cancel or move companies:** reason (Not enough participants, Trainer not available, Other, Venue not available) and a few words. **Move companies to another run** (same title listed first; warns for a different title; refuses if the seats there aren't enough): open deals move now; won deals get a Change date request carrying the new run (`trainingChange.newRunId`), and approving it moves the deal onto that run. **Cancel, no new date:** open deals become lost ("Training cancelled", the words as the note); won deals get a **Cancel the training** request a manager approves. The run is marked Cancelled (with where its companies went).
- **Not changed by a run:** each company's funding type, value, pax and owner. Changing the list price later doesn't change companies already in.

## Batch 2, part B: money and reports (2026-10-28)

Joel: "Split by pax".

- **Each company's share of the run's costs, by pax:** a run's costs (approved / paid, in the P&L, as usual) are shared out to its **won** companies by their pax (4 of 7 pax = 57.1% of each cost; equally if none has pax; the shares of one cost add up to it exactly, to the sen). Worked out when the P&L is calculated, never saved (`runCostShares`), so a company joining, leaving or changing pax re-splits by itself. It reaches everything that uses `projectRows`: Project P&L, Payment tracking, the weekly presentation, the backup's P&L tab, the rep's GP % KPI and the GP alerts (server, `serverKpi.ts`); plus a deal's Money out and P&L panel (`useRunShares`) and My home's Money position. A run's cost left out of the P&L is flagged once, not per company.
- **On the company's deal:** Money out and P&L says "Public programme: costs include this company's share of the programme's costs, RM … (…%, by pax). They are raised and paid once on the programme."
- **One P&L per run:** Deals → Public programmes → the run → **P&L for this programme** (Admin and Management): income, SST / HRDC % / commission, costs, profit and GP % for all won companies together, and one line per company (pax, income, its costs, profit). Project P&L keeps one row per company (the run's costs counted once in its totals, split across those rows).
- **Training report:** the trainer writes one report on the run (in the portal or on the run's Training report step); a new report starts with every company's T3 names. Each company's deal (After training) shows **Training report · From the public programme** with **PDF for HR** and **PDF for HOD** once it is checked on the run: the copy keeps only that company's people, matched by name to its T3 list (attendance absent / late, outstanding, needs attention, follow-ups, scores, notes per person), shows its pax as registered, its name as the client and its HRDC approval code; the summary, objectives, observations, topics, photos and actions stay. Without a T3 list no person is printed. (`companyReport`, `allNames`, `CompanyReportBox` in `DealChecklist.tsx`.) The HR link (`/t/`) for a company copy isn't built: PDF only.
- **Nothing to publish** (P&L data is read by Admin and Management as before; reps still never see costs).
- **Tested:** 15 logic checks (only won, not deleted companies; shares add up to the sen; 4 of 7 = RM 1,714.29 of RM 3,000; equal split without pax; no won companies = no shares; other deals untouched; company P&L costs = its shares; run P&L = the companies added up; a company's report copy keeps only its people and pax, shared parts kept, original untouched; no T3 = no names); test CRM: the run's P&L showed RM 10,500 income, RM 4,400 costs, RM 5,552.20 profit (GP 52.9%), Valmont RM 2,514.29 and Padini RM 1,885.71; Valmont's deal showed its 57.1% share line and the report box ("2 on its T3 list") and its PDF for HR was made.

## Costing estimator for public programmes (2026-10-28)

Joel: "there should be a costing estimator for public class as well", and "remember the variation of different fee for different companies in the same class".

- **Where:** Deals → Costing estimator → **New public programme estimate** (everyone), or Deals → Public programmes → the run → **Cost this programme** (Admin and Management, since it shows other reps' prices). From a run it opens that run's estimate if there is one, else makes one and saves it straight away. It is a mock P&L only: it never changes a run, deal or quotation.
- **Companies:** each option lists the companies in the class, each with its own pax, fee per pax (before SST) and an HRDC tick; a fee under the list price shows "x% below list". HRDC keeps its % (default `hrdcDeductionPct`, on the amount incl. SST) only of the HRDC companies' fees. Total pax, average fee per pax, seat limit and minimum warnings.
- **Options A to D** are different mixes (e.g. Bookings so far, Expected, Full room); a new option starts as a copy of the one shown. From a run: A = the companies on it now (pax, fee = deal value ÷ pax, HRDC for HRDC and HRDC + top-up deals), B = A plus the open seats at list price (when there is a seat limit); **Option A: companies on the programme now** refreshes A later. **Fill the N open seats at list price** adds a line.
- **Costs:** each line is **RM per run** (trainer, venue hire, facilitator, marketing), **RM per pax** (meals, materials, certificates: × total pax) or **% of net sales** (commission). The one-client estimator also has RM per pax now (× its pax).
- **Shows:** the P&L for the option shown, GP and GP % (company GP bands), the **break-even head-count** at that option's average fee and HRDC mix (or "Never" when each pax costs more than they bring), the **average fee per pax** for a target GP % at that head-count (rounded up to RM 10), and all options side by side (★ = highest GP).
- **Saved** in `cost_estimates` like any estimate (`kind: 'public'`, `runId`, `listPrice`, `seatLimit`, `minPax`, `options`). Nothing to publish (the rule only checks the owner).
- **Files:** `lib/costEstimate.ts` (`publicResult`, `feeForTarget`, `blankPublicEstimate`), `components/PublicEstimateEditor.tsx`, `components/EstimateParts.tsx` (shared with `CostEstimator.tsx`), Cost this programme in `PublicProgrammes.tsx`.
- **Tested:** 20 maths checks (HRDC only on HRDC companies, SST, per pax lines, commission on net sales, break-even, target fee reaches the target, one-client estimates unchanged). Test CRM: a run with Valmont (6 pax at RM 1,200, HRDC) and Padini (3 at RM 1,500, direct): A 9 pax, RM 11,700, GP RM 4,129.64 (35.3%), break-even 6 pax; B full room 20 pax, GP 62.9%; 40% target → about RM 1,420 per pax; saved and reopened from the run; phone width fine.

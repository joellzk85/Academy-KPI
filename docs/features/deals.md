# Deals: journey, appointments, sales flow, follow-ups, on hold, duplicates

Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-21. Detailed history of what was built and agreed. Read only when working on this area.

- **Editing appointments and deleting deals (Joel, 2026-10-16):** only the **person who created the deal** (`creatorId`, falling back to the owner for old deals) **or Management** (`canManageDeal` in `lib/deals.ts`). They can change a booked appointment, edit a logged one (older ticked ones too), and **Delete deal** (bottom of the deal panel). A deal can't be deleted if it's won, has a **sent** quotation, money records, HRDC queries or proposal files; use Mark lost instead. Since 2026-10-18 deleting **keeps** the deal (see "Follow-ups, lost reasons and deleted deals"); its draft quotations and course outlines are hidden with it, and a rep can't delete if one of them belongs to someone else (the rules only let reps change their own or shared ones).
  - **Red dot on the Deals menu (fixed 2026-10-17):** it used an old check across the whole company's deals. It now uses `dealNeedsAttention` in `lib/deals.ts`, the same check as the red rows on the Deals list, for the person's own deals only, plus "Help asked" when a teammate tagged them. The rules enforce: delete only by the creator or Management, never a won deal (rules published by Joel on 2026-10-17).
- **Deal panel and checklist (Joel, 2026-10-17):**
  - "Client agreed" is now shown as **"Client approved"** everywhere. The stored stage value is still `agreed` (and `agreedDate`).
  - The journey line shows **the date each step was done** under it (`stageDate` in `lib/deals.ts`). Older deals without a saved date show nothing.
  - The deal details show **Venue**: the checklist's venue, else the quotation's, marked "(from quotation)" (`dealVenue` in `lib/checklist.ts`).
  - **Check venue + payment** and **Confirm trainer** start with the quotation's venue and trainer(s) (`effectiveVenue`, `effectiveTrainer`). They use the quotation marked sent, else the newest draft, and list every trainer on its programme lines.
  - Admin can pick from the list or type (`ChosenBox` in `DealChecklist.tsx`, saved as `venueId`/`venueName` and `trainerId`/`trainerName`), or "Use the quotation's venue" to go back.
  - Ticking Done saves whatever came from the quotation onto the checklist, so later quotation changes don't alter it. `PickOrType` is now its own file, shared with quotations.
  - **Side panels are half the screen** on computers (`md:w-1/2 md:min-w-[40rem]`) and full width on phones (Joel, 2026-10-17). This covers the Deals panel and edit form, the Work queue deal panel, the Clients panel and form, and the Programmes panel.
- **Deal journey (Joel, 2026-10-16): a proposal is not a quotation.** The **proposal** is the pitch. The **quotation** is sent only after the client agrees. **Won = the client signed the quotation.**
  - Stages: `lead` (New) → `proposal` (Proposal sent; KPI "Proposal" counts these) → `agreed` (Client agreed; `agreedDate`) → `quoted` (Quotation sent; `quotationSentDate`) → `won` (`wonDate`; Closing RM counts here) / `lost`. `appointment` is still a stage value but means "new, has met", and is shown as New on the journey line (`JOURNEY`, `journeyStage` in `lib/deals.ts`).
  - The appointment can happen at any point before Won (before or after the proposal); the appointment box works at every open stage.
  - Main button by stage: New → "Proposal sent"; Proposal sent → "Client approved: create quotation"; Client approved → "Create quotation" / "Open quotation" (plus "Quotation sent another way" if one exists); Quotation sent → "Client signed". "Mark lost" is a small link at every open stage. Won can only be marked from Quotation sent. (Updated 2026-10-18, see "Smoother sales flow".)
  - Quotations: "Create quotation" only appears from Client agreed (`canQuote`). The New quotation picker lists only agreed/quoted/won deals; the "client only" option was removed. Marking a quotation **sent** moves its deal from Client agreed to Quotation sent automatically.
  - Deals tiles and Management's pipeline card use New, Proposal sent, Client agreed, Quotation sent. Quotations unsigned for 7+ days with no follow-up count as stuck.
- **Appointments on deals (2026-10-16, Joel):** an appointment is **optional**; reps may go straight from Lead to "Proposal sent". **At most one appointment per deal**, stored on the deal as `appointment` (`src/lib/appointments.ts`, UI `src/components/DealAppointmentBox.tsx` in the deal panel). The old one-click "Appointment held" button is gone.
  - **Book** (date, time, face-to-face/online/call): shows on the **team calendar**. App builds calendar entries from deals (`appointmentEvents`), ids `appt_<dealId>`, which can't be deleted from the calendar.
  - **Log** (one form): meeting date (not in the future), met with, type; **what the client wants is saved straight onto the deal** (programmeId/courseName, trainingDate, `participants`, proposalValue, fundingType); outcome, next step and follow-up date are **required**. Logging sets `appointmentDate` (KPI counts it then, once per deal), moves Lead to Appointment, and writes history.
  - Accountability: booked and not logged a day after the date = red on the deal row, in My home "My follow-ups" and in Management's "stuck deals". Logged more than 3 days late shows "logged N days late". New My home card **"Team appointments this week"** (Admin and Management; default for Management).
  - Quotations read participants from the deal when it's set (single source).

- **Smoother sales flow (built 2026-10-18; Joel: reps left the deal to do the quotation, had to find their way back, and clicked through steps that added nothing):**
  - **Rep stays inside the deal.** "Create quotation" on a deal opens it straight in the editor (`handleCreateQuotationForPipeline` in `RepDetailDashboard.tsx`): if the deal already has a draft, that draft opens (no duplicates); otherwise a linked draft is saved (next free reference for the day) and opened. The editor shows **"Back to deal"** instead of "Quotations" when opened from a deal (`backToDeal` on `Editor`; `openFromDeal`/`onBackToDeal` on `QuotationsPage`). After a send (Email, WhatsApp, or Download PDF → Mark as sent), **closing the "Sent" box** returns to Deals with that deal's panel open (`openDealId` on `DealsBoard`). It waits for the close, not the instant of sending, because on phones the "PDF ready: share now" fallback sits in that box. The Admin work queue's "Create quotation" is unchanged.
  - **Details asked for when they matter** (`dealGaps` in `lib/deals.ts`): a **new deal** needs only the client, what they asked for (programme from the list **or** a short typed note, saved as `enquiryNote`, never in `courseName`) and the date asked. Value and funding type are optional there. **Proposal sent** (and "Client approved: create quotation") needs the programme from the list and the deal value. **Won** needs the client, the value and the funding type. When a step is blocked, a small box (`GapsBox` in `src/components/DealGates.tsx`) asks only for the missing fields, saves them (history "Added …") and carries on with the step. The won box asks for missing ones next to the won date. Older deals keep their typed `courseName` when edited. `dealAskedFor` shows the programme name, else "Asked for: <note>" (deal list, panel, search).
  - **"Client approved" merged into "Create quotation":** from Proposal sent the main button is **"Client approved: create quotation"**. One click saves `stage: 'agreed'`, `agreedDate` = today, then opens the new quotation. Stored stage values are unchanged, so KPIs, reports and old deals don't change; the journey line still shows Client approved.
  - **Mark won where the rep is:** at Quotation sent the deal shows **"Waiting for client signature"** with **"Client signed"** as the main button. A sent quotation has **"Client signed: mark deal won"** in its Sent banner, and Deals list rows at Quotation sent have a small **"Client signed"** button under the row. All three use `MarkWonBox` + `markDealWon` (`DealGates.tsx`): same won-date box and checks, same people (deal owner, Admin, Management), and the admin checklist starts (it checks first whether one already exists).
  - **Deal panel: next step first.** Under the client name and journey line is one **Next step** box (the single main button, plus "Mark lost" / "Reopen deal" / "Undo win" as small links). Everything else folds into sections (`Fold` in `DealsBoard.tsx`): Appointment, Details (with Edit details), Documents and proposal files, Admin checklist, HRDC queries, Money / Payment status, History. The open one depends on the stage (`defaultSection`): New → Appointment; Proposal sent, Client approved, Quotation sent → Documents; Won → Admin checklist; Lost → History.
  - **KPI counting unchanged** (Proposal on `proposalSentDate`, Appointment when logged, Closing RM on `wonDate`). **No database rules change.**
  - **Tested** with a temporary local page (the real rep workspace on a fake in-memory database), desktop and phone, new deal to won, plus older deals, the list button and the quotation button. Clicks from New deal to Won, same details typed, quotation sent via Download PDF → Mark as sent: **before 21 clicks, 9 screen changes; after 16 clicks, 5 screen changes** (one fewer each way by email).
  - **Quotation editor on phones (fixed 2026-10-18):** it used to be about 850px wide on a phone, so the whole page and the bottom bar scrolled sideways. Cause: the editor's sections sit in a `<fieldset>` (used to lock a sent quotation), and browsers make a fieldset at least as wide as its content, so the programme table's 800px pushed everything wider. Fix: `min-w-0` on that fieldset (`QuotationsPage.tsx`). Now the page fits at 375px with 16px gutters, the bottom bar's buttons wrap, and the programme table scrolls sideways inside its own card on narrow screens. Wide computer screens look the same; a 1024px screen no longer scrolls sideways either. Tested with a temporary local page (draft and sent, 375 / 1024 / 1440px). A stacked card layout for programme lines on phones was left for later.
  - **Pick lists close on a click elsewhere (Joel, 2026-10-18):** the deal form's Client and "What they asked for" (programme) search lists used to stay open until something was picked. They now close on a click or tab anywhere outside them, or Esc (`useCloseOnOutside` in `src/lib/useCloseOnOutside.ts`, used by `ClientPicker` in `DealsBoard.tsx` and `ProgrammePicker` in `Programmes.tsx`). Typed search text stays; after "Change", clicking away keeps the earlier choice. Use the same hook for any new drop-down list.

- **Follow-ups, lost reasons and deleted deals (built 2026-10-18; Joel's spec, with his yes to two details):**
  - **Follow-up rules** (Management → **Follow-up rules**, `FollowUpRulesPage.tsx`; saved in `config/follow_up_rules`, rule: members read, Management write). Per stage a list of checkpoints in days, each stage can be switched off. Defaults: **New deal 15, 30, 45** (from the date the client asked, `requestDate`; "appointment" counts as New), **Proposal sent 15, 30, 45** (from `proposalSentDate`), **Quotation sent 15, 30, 45** (from `quotationSentDate`). **Client approved has no checkpoints** (Joel agreed). The clock starts on the day the deal reached the stage; logging a note doesn't restart it.
  - **Logic** is in `src/lib/followUps.ts` (pure, no database code, because the server shares `lib/deals.ts`): `followUpAlert(deal, today)` → "1st / 2nd / 3rd follow-up due" when a checkpoint has passed and no note is dated on or after it; after the **last** checkpoint → **"Decision needed"** until a **Keep open** note (reason + next follow-up date) is logged on or after it. If that Keep open's next date passes, **"Decision needed again"** (Joel agreed). The live rules are loaded once in `App.tsx` (`useFollowUpRules` in `src/lib/useFollowUpRules.ts`, which calls `setActiveFollowUpRules`).
  - **Notes on the deal:** `deal.followUps[]` = `{ id, date, how (call/whatsapp/email/visit/other), text, nextDate, at, byId, byName, stage, decision?: 'keep_open', reason? }` (last 200). Logging also sets `followUpDate` and adds a History line. UI: `src/components/FollowUps.tsx` (`FollowUpForm`, `FollowUpList`). In the deal panel's Next step box: the red alert line with **Log follow-up** (or **Keep open** + **Mark lost** at a decision), and a small "Log follow-up" link otherwise; the **Follow-ups** section lists every note, newest first, "Followed up N times" (opens first when there's an alert).

## 6a. Phase 2C, HRDC work (built 2026-10-16)

Joel's input: HRDC can raise **queries** before the training is approved, during the training, and after it's completed, and the team must attend to them. Some new programmes must be **pre-registered in the HRDC portal** and approved before they can be used for a client. Joel said "let's move on" without answering two detail questions, so these defaults were agreed (with his yes):
- Programmes live in one list (Admin work → Programmes). Anyone can quick-add a programme from the deal form, but only as "Not registered". Only Admin and Management change its HRDC status, code and dates. Deals must pick a programme from the list. Older deals keep their typed name until edited.
- HRDC query reply-by dates are typed from HRDC's email for each query, with no hardcoded deadline. "Handled by" can be any active team member and defaults to the person logging it. Admin and Management add, answer, reopen or delete queries. Reps see them read-only.
- Gate: an HRDC or HRDC + top-up deal whose programme isn't HRDC approved (or isn't picked from the list) can't tick "Apply grant". The deal panel shows why.
- Work queue: an open query is a red flag, an overdue query goes to the top, and a deal with open queries isn't "All done". A programme that isn't approved is an amber flag while the grant step is open. Programmes → "Waiting on HRDC" lists submitted programmes.

- "Verify T3 attendance" is now a built-in after-training, HRDC-only step with a file upload (2026-10-19, see section 6).
- **Programmes, course outlines and trainers belong together (Joel, 2026-10-17: "program list, course outline, trainer topics should co-relate"):** the Programme list is the master.
  - **Course outlines** have an optional `programmeId` (Joel: optional). The editor's "Programme" box picks from the list; blank = use the linked deal's programme, or none for a general outline. The course title follows the programme's name (`useProgrammeName`) unless "Type a different title". New outlines take the deal's (or the copied outline's) programme. "Start from" puts outlines of the same programme first. Logic: `outlineProgrammeId` and `resolveOutline` in `lib/outlineResolve.ts`.

  - **Admin work → Programmes:** each row shows deals · outlines · trainers, and the panel lists the programme's course outlines and the trainers who teach it. Delete is refused while deals or outlines use the programme; trainers who teach it get it unticked.
  - **Trainer pickers** (quotation lines, the checklist's Confirm trainer step) list the trainers who teach that programme first, marked "Teaches this programme" (`trainerOptions` in `PickOrType.tsx`).

## Trainers' personal details are private (process audit, 2026-10-22)

- Gender, address, emergency contact, race, nationality and rate live in `trainer_private/{trainerId}` (Admin and Management only), with the same field names; `PRIVATE_TRAINER_FIELDS` and helpers in `lib/trainerProfile.ts`.
- **Staff:**
  - Trainers merges them into each trainer, and saves split them.
  - `moveTrainerPrivate` moves older records once per page load and removes the fields from `trainers_directory`.
- **Reps:** they don't see these fields (hidden in the list, panel, form and review). The rules refuse a rep write that touches them.
- **Profile-link submissions:**
  - The six fields go to `trainer_link_private/{token}`; the link only says `privateSent`.
  - A staff accept copies them to `trainer_private`.
  - A rep's accept leaves a `privateLinkToken` on the trainer, and the next staff visit moves the fields.
- **Server:** the trainer portal reads and writes them in `trainer_private` (`trainerFrom` merges them in; the profile save and payment address write there).
- **Known gap:** submissions sent before this change still hold the fields on `trainer_links` until they are accepted or cancelled.

## Programmes linked to course outlines and trainers (2026-10-23)

Joel: "the programme approved is not linked to the course outline." Sometimes the client asks for a programme first and the outline comes later; sometimes an outline's title is the programme itself; sometimes an outline suits a programme with a different name.

- **Admin work → Programmes → a programme:** link course outlines (search by title, ref or company; an outline keeps its own title, so one with a different name can be linked; one linked elsewhere asks before moving), **upload outline files** (PDF, Word or PowerPoint, kept on the programme as `outlineFiles`, storage `programme_files/{programmeId}/...`, Admin and Management upload and remove, everyone opens), and link or unlink trainers (writes the trainer's `programmeIds`). A new programme stays open after saving so these can be linked straight away. Code: `ProgrammeLinks.tsx`.
- An outline points to one programme (`programmeId`). Linking a draft keeps the title it shows now; it follows the programme's name only if they are the same.
- **Deal → What they asked for:** clicking the box shows the whole programme list by category; typing narrows it and also finds course outlines. An outline linked to a programme picks that programme; an unlinked one can become the programme (its title becomes the name, and the outline is linked to it when the person may edit that outline). Typing a new name and "Add" still adds a programme. Once picked, the programme's outlines, outline files and trainers show under it.

## 6b. Phase 3, money (built 2026-10-16)

Joel's answers (2026-10-16):
- HRDC deals: after training, HR signs the JD14, T3 attendance is verified, we invoice (claim) HRDC, and HRDC pays and notifies us by email.
- **HRDC always keeps 4%** and pays the rest. This is expected, not a shortfall to invoice the client for.
- Not fully HRDC (direct or top-up): invoice the client directly **before** training; the client pays **after** training.
- Money out: Admin or Management can request payment. The cross-approval rule is confirmed. Some **deposits are paid before training** (venue, bus, materials).

What was built:
- `money_in/{id}`: HRDC claim or client invoice on a deal (number, date, amount; then paid date and amount). Admin and Management record it. Everyone can read it, because reps see payment status only (`RepMoneyStatus`).
- `money_out/{id}`: payment request (category, payee, amount, timing "Deposit before training" or "After training"). Statuses: pending → approved or rejected → paid. Admin and Management only.
  - The rules enforce that only a manager who did not raise the request can approve or reject it, so an Admin request can be decided by either manager and a manager's request only by the other. Only approved items can be marked paid. The UI hides Approve on your own request.
- `config/money_settings`: `hrdcDeductionPct` (default 4), `overdueDays` (default 30), `claimDeadlineDays` (default 180: HRD Corp allows 6 months; blank = no countdown), `claimReminderDays` (default 14: red reminder if the claim isn't submitted by then).
- **One P&L only (Joel, 2026-10-21: "both are the same, make sure there are no duplication"):** profit is worked out in one place, `projectRow` in `src/lib/projectPL.ts` (income, minus SST, HRDC %, commission and counted costs). The deal's "Money out and P&L" section, Management → Project P&L and the My home Money position card all use it, including an SST amount typed in Project P&L (Admin can read that once `firestore.rules` is published). `dealMoneySums` in `src/lib/money.ts` only adds up invoiced, received, still to come in, still to pay out and waiting for approval; it has no profit of its own. The old per-rep "P&L sheets" code (`project_pls`) was deleted from `RepDetailDashboard.tsx`; its old data stays listed in Data tools as "Old P&L sheets (no longer used)" to clear.
- Work queue flags (`moneyFlags`): HRDC claim not submitted after training (with deadline countdown once set), client invoice not sent (amber within 7 days of training, red after), payment overdue, HRDC paid less than expected, requests waiting for approval. A deal is "All done" only when its checklist is done, its queries are answered and its money is settled.
- Screens: "Money" section on won deals (Deals panel and work queue). Admin work → Money lists approvals waiting, approved-to-pay, money still to come in, and the settings.
- `src/components/Money.tsx` and `src/lib/money.ts`.
- **Company payments (Joel, 2026-10-18: "sometimes payment requests are required outside of training"):** Admin work → Money → **Company payments** → **Raise a company payment** (Admin and Management). Fields: date of request (`requestDate`, defaults to today), category (`COMPANY_CATEGORIES`: Office, Rental, Utilities, Marketing, Equipment, Software and subscriptions, Travel, Staff claim, Other), pay to, **items** (`items[]` of description, qty, price; `amount` = their total, `itemsTotal`), info (`description`), needed by (optional), payment details (optional, e.g. bank and account).
  - Stored in `money_out` with `dealId: ''` and `kind: 'company'` (`isCompanyPayment`), so the **same approval rules** apply (either manager approves an Admin's request; managers cross-approve; never your own; only approved can be marked paid). No rules change was needed.
  - They show in "Waiting for approval" and "Approved, not paid yet" labelled "Company payment", and in the Company payments list (all statuses, newest first, month filter, paid / to pay / waiting totals). Amounts show sen (`formatSen`; since 2026-10-20 `formatRM` does too).
  - My home: Management's Approvals card and Admin's Money jobs label them "Company payment"; the Money position card's "Approved, to pay" and "Waiting for approval" include them.
  - **Not** in any deal's P&L or Project P&L (they filter by `dealId`). The backup already includes them (Money out tab).
